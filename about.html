About Our Night Hotel 🦉

Our Mission

NightOwl Care was founded to help tourists experience the city's nightlife with complete peace of mind. Whether you are attending a late-night dinner, a concert, or a theater show, we ensure your children and pets are safe, happy, and asleep in a secure hotel environment.

Safety First

Our hotel is monitored 24/7 by certified night nannies and professional pet caretakers. We require ID/Passport verification upon check-in, and you will receive live updates directly to your phone throughout the night.
